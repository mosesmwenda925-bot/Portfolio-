About

I'm Moses, based in Gatanga along the Thika–Gatanga road. I design logos, posters, flyers, and social media posts for small businesses — and I build websites too.

My approach starts with understanding the business, not just making something look good — every design needs to actually help sell, promote, or communicate. From there: structure, type, color, motion, in that order.

Currently open to freelance design and web work.
